# Code of Conduct

Proton Security exists to help people who are new to Linux. We want the project itself to be just as welcoming.

## Our standards

In issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, we:

- are kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first language isn't English;
- assume good intentions and ask before we judge;
- give feedback about the work, never about the person;
- accept decisions we disagree with once they are made, and keep arguing for change respectfully;
- respect people's privacy and never publish someone's private information.

We don't accept harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualized content, threats, or repeated
unwanted contact, whatever the reason.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately at
[support@protonos.org](mailto:support@protonos.org), or report the comment with GitHub's *Report content* menu.
Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban people who
break this code, depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is.
